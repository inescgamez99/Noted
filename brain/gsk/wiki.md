## 10. Project context

**Client:** GlaxoSmithKline (GSK) — External Manufacturing (EM) division
**Engagement partner:** Accenture (UK) Limited
**Active contracts:** SOW 528 (NPI System Reinvention, Jun–Sep 2026, £192k); SOW 530 (Agentic Reinvention Kaizen, Jun–Jul 2026, £79k); further SOWs for Agentic Factory and Orchestration Platform MVP in discussion

**Programme:** EM NPI Launch System Reinvention — part of GSK's internal "Pipeline to Patient Transformation"

**Objective:** Reinvent External Manufacturing's New Product Introduction process to double the number of launches (from 4 to 6–8 per year), reduce cycle time by 50%, and achieve consistent Right First Time (RFT) delivery, supporting £40bn revenue and 20 asset launches through 2032

**Scope:** 7 core NPI systems — SIA, Team Formation & Charter, Plan & Schedule, PPQ Preparation, PPQ Execution, EHS & Quality Audit, File/Approval/Launch — plus 14 enabling systems (governance, people, finance, risk, PMO, comms, CI, and others)

**Approach:** Three-phase model: (1) Standardise — 80% SM anchor model, (2) Optimise — DS/DP enrichment (+10–15%), (3) Digitise — agentic AI and automation overlay (−25–30% manual effort). Kaizen workshops are the primary design vehicle. AI architecture uses a two-tier model: Utility Agents for repeatable tasks, Specialist Agents for orchestration-level reasoning.

**Key measures:** PPQ RFT rate (baseline ~20%), NPI cycle time, launches per year, hours saved, FTE capacity freed
